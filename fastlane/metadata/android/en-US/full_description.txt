<p>RadioPult is a remote control for Wi-Fi / internet radios with a Frontier Silicon module, the radios that are normally controlled with the <b>UNDOK</b> app. If UNDOK no longer works for you, RadioPult controls the same radios over your home Wi-Fi.</p>

<p>Frontier Silicon radios are sold by many brands, for example Hama, Medion, Roberts, Technisat, Sangean, Silvercrest, Blaupunkt and Auna. Any radio that works with UNDOK should work with RadioPult.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>First-time setup: bring a new radio from its setup Wi-Fi into your home Wi-Fi and name it, also via WPS, network cable or fixed IP</li>
<li>Finds radios on the network automatically, or enter the IP address</li>
<li>Power, source (internet radio, DAB, FM, Bluetooth, AUX …), volume and mute, also with the phone's volume buttons</li>
<li>Play / stop, next / previous, shuffle, repeat, seek, station name, track info and details, station logo</li>
<li>FM tuning with frequency display</li>
<li>Presets: recall, save the current station or any station from the list, rename, change the stream address, delete, reorder</li>
<li>Station list for internet radio and DAB with search and DAB scan, CD track list, USB browsing, context menus and sign-in forms</li>
<li>Multiroom groups and stereo pairs</li>
<li>Play music from the phone on the radio</li>
<li>Bluetooth pairing</li>
<li>Optional lock-screen controls</li>
<li>Equalizer presets, custom bass / treble and loudness</li>
<li>Settings: device and network info, rename, language, sleep timer, date, time and time zone, stream quality, speaker delay, find radio, factory reset</li>
<li>Remembers the last radio and its PIN and reconnects on start</li>
</ul>

<p>No account, no ads, no tracking. The app talks to the radio directly in your local network.</p>

<p>Not included: alarms, firmware updates, Spotify Connect and other streaming apps.</p>
